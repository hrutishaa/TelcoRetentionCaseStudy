# Gap Analysis

| Area | Current state | Future state | Gap | Action |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Detection | Churn found at cancellation call | Weekly risk flag for first-year month-to-month customers | No risk scoring | Build rule-based score from contract, tenure, payment, add-ons (BR-1) |
| Ownership | Nobody owns first-year customers | Retention queue owned by Care | No queue or SLA | Create queue, 7-day contact target (BR-2) |
| Offers | Ad-hoc discounts | Offers matched to the churn reason | No offer catalogue | Marketing defines 3–4 standard offers (BR-3) |
| Payment | 45.3% churn on electronic check vs 15–17% on autopay | Autopay pushed at onboarding | No nudge | Autopay credit at signup (BR-6) |
| Add-ons | 49.0% churn with no tech support/security vs 9.0% with both | Trials offered to at-risk customers | Not offered proactively | Include trial in offer set |
| Measurement | Saves not tracked | Saved MRR vs control group | No outcome logging | Outcome codes + monthly report (BR-4, BR-5) |
